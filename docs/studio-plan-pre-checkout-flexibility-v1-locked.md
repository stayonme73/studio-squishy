# Studio Plan — Pre-Checkout Flexibility V1

**LOCKED** · Do not modify without Tagia approval.

## Principle

> Customers can freely build and refine their Studio Plan before checkout. Checkout confirms the selected scope. After checkout, additions or removals are managed through the Project Change process.

## Customer expectation

**Before payment** — build, add, remove, change, explore. Nothing is finalized until checkout is complete.

**After payment** — purchased scope is established. Further additions or removals are Project Changes, not silent edits.
