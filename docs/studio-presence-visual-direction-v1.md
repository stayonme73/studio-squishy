# Studio Presence Visual Direction v1

**Status:** Active direction · **2026-07-18**  
**Scope:** Illusion of an invisible Studio representative — not feature work, not frame redesign.

The current direction is close. The remaining work is not about adding features. It is about creating the illusion that an invisible Studio representative is standing here.

## 1. Eyes must feel recessed behind the lenses

The eyes should not read as graphics attached to the glasses.

- Create visible depth between the lenses and the eyes.
- Lower the eyes slightly inside the frames.
- Push them visually behind the lenses.
- Allow the upper eyelid to naturally overlap approximately 10% of the iris.
- Add a very subtle lens reflection so the visual order becomes: **glasses → lens → eyes**.

The viewer should immediately perceive the glasses first, then notice someone looking through them.

## 2. Soften the eye opening

Replace pointed openings with a softer relaxed almond shape.

- Rounded inner and outer corners
- Fuller upper eyelid
- Softer lower lid
- No sharp cat-eye appearance

Goal: calm attentiveness, not exaggerated expression.

## 3. Integrate the eyebrows

Brows should belong to an invisible person — not floating objects.

- Thinner
- Closer to the glasses
- Subtle curvature
- Minimal movement

They should disappear into the overall illusion until expression requires them.

## 4. Relax the expression

The Presence should feel composed, patient, and quietly attentive.

Neutral expression:

- Upper lid covers approximately 10–15% of the iris
- Visible white remains around the iris
- Pupils remain centered unless intentionally tracking something

Feeling: **"I'm listening."** — not **"I'm staring."**

## 5. Lock the glasses

The frame has reached a strong direction. **Stop redesigning the glasses.**

Treat the current frame as the foundation. Future effort improves believability, not silhouette.

Identity evolves through: eye construction · eyelids · motion · depth · expression — **not** repeated frame redesigns.

## 6. Build the illusion of an invisible person

**Primary design objective.**

The viewer should gradually stop seeing *glasses + eyes* and begin feeling *someone is standing here.*

Created through:

- Subtle eye movement
- Natural blinking
- Restrained expressions
- Eyes that appear to exist behind the lenses
- Gentle breathing and floating motion
- Small pauses and attention shifts

## Attention philosophy (working, not posing)

- **Idle:** soft look-around the Studio — quietly doing the job, not waiting for applause.
- **Customer interaction:** attention turns to them.
- **Tablet / wall display:** eyes lead to the task first.
- Never locked in a constant direct stare (chatbot-waiting feel).
