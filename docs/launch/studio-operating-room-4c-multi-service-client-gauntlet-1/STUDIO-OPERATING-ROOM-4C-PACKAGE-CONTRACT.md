## Objective

Prove The Studio can truthfully **accept and fulfill** realistic customer requests that combine multiple currently approved Launch Now services — without contradicting the brief, losing brand/campaign continuity, promising unsupported work, hiding limits, depending on owner production labor, producing disconnected assets, or falsely declaring customer-ready.

This is **not** a single-asset capability test.

## Shared authoritative brief

Each scenario must have **one** authoritative customer brief. All deliverables read from that brief (or a derived immutable campaign-facts record). Conflicting copies of offer, audience, tone, CTA, dates, contact, or sizes are defects.

---

## Pre-acceptance truth checks (A)

Before production begins, Machine must:

1. Confirm every requested deliverable is on the frozen Launch Now menu.  
2. Disclose explicit limits for each accepted service.  
3. Check rights, timing, clarity, safety, and capability.  
4. Reject, narrow, or clarify unsupported asks — never pretend.

---

## Supported-scope enforcement

Unsupported requests (carousel, ad ops, vendor-editor labor, invented SKUs) must be refused or narrowed **before** acceptance. Acceptance that silently expands scope is a FAIL.

---

## Cross-service fact consistency (B)

Offer, audience, tone, CTA, dates, contact, and required sizes must be consistent across deliverables. Contradictions detected before production are blocked; contradictions found after production are defects.

---

## Machine routing expectations (C)

The Machine routes the accepted request into the correct production paths for each service. Shared campaign facts are reused — not retyped into conflicting versions. Each service must complete without losing connection to the overall campaign.

---

## Owner-production-independence test (D)

Production must complete without Tagia manually designing, editing, formatting, or repairing deliverables. Record every manual owner intervention, even small. Owner creative approval does **not** count as production labor.

---

## Coordinated campaign-quality test (E)

Deliverables must look and read like one coordinated campaign, be commercially usable (not merely generated), use correct formats/dimensions, and have clear naming, packaging, versioning, and delivery.

---

## File-format and dimension checks

Per expected-output matrix. Missing or wrong format/size is a quality defect.

---

## Mobile / responsive observations (F)

Maintain responsive/mobile coverage while testing hire/submit, communicate, track, feedback, approve, and receive delivery. Do **not** attempt final dedicated mobile certification in Room 4C. Record defects and risks for the later Room 4 mobile-certification gate (or Room 4D as Tagia directs).
