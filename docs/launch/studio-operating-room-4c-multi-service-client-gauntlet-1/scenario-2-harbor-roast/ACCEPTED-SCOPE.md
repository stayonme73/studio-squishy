# Accepted scope — Scenario 2

Accepted Launch Now services only:

- Campaign creative (coordinator + written direction)
- Social graphics (square PNG 1080×1080 and vertical PNG 1080×1920)
- Short-form video (vertical MP4)
- Marketing copy / email (paste-ready email + caption)
- Print collateral (5×7 in counter card; PNG 1500×2100 at 300 DPI; PDF 360×504 pt)

Refused before production:

- Carousel — not on Launch Now menu
- Ad account management / targeting / budgets / ops
- Unsupported sizes outside Studio contracts
- Invented shop URL, contact email, or phone (none was owner-authorized)
- Invented origin, weight, or flavor claims (none was supplied)
