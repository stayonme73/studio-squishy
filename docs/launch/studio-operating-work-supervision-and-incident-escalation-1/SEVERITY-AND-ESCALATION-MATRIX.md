# Severity and escalation matrix (opening)

| Severity | When | Owner interruption | Presentation |
|----------|------|--------------------|--------------|
| `ROUTINE` | Recoverable without judgment | No, if recovery works | Console Watchkeeper allowed |
| `CUSTOMER_DELAY_RISK` | Customer wait growing | If recovery fails or delay is material | Serious, complete record |
| `DEADLINE_CRITICAL` | Promised date at risk | Yes if Machine cannot recover in time | Serious, complete record |
| `FINANCIAL_RISK` | Money, refund, or payment truth | Yes | Serious, complete record |
| `RIGHTS_OR_COMPLIANCE_RISK` | Rights, likeness, or compliance hold | Yes when Owner judgment is required | Serious, complete record |
| `SECURITY_SUSPECTED` | Possible breach or unauthorized access | Immediate | **No Squishy.** Direct containment copy |
| `SECURITY_CONFIRMED` | Confirmed security incident | Immediate | **No Squishy.** Direct containment copy |

Escalate to Tagia only after recovery fails, or when Owner judgment is genuinely required, or for financial, rights, or security classes above.

If Tagia does nothing, the incident record must say what the Machine will do next and when it checks again.
