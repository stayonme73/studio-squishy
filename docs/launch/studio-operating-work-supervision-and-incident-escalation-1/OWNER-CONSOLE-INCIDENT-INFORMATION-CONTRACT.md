# Owner Console incident-information contract (opening)

The Console must be an incident command view, not a toast.

For every escalated incident Tagia must see, in plain language:

1. What happened  
2. Which customer and project  
3. Who or what stalled  
4. Who or what caused it, only when evidence supports that  
5. Current customer, deadline, and security impact  
6. Recovery already attempted  
7. Who is responsible now  
8. Who should be contacted  
9. What Tagia must decide or do (or that she need do nothing)  
10. What happens if she does nothing  
11. When the Machine checks again  
12. Evidence and full history  

Today’s Owner Console is a **decision desk** for production exceptions. It does not yet meet this contract. Implementation is not in this opening.

Security suspected/confirmed: serious layout, no Squishy.
