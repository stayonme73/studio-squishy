## Findings that are **not** a close

These belong on the Manager gate. They did **not** count branded email as fail.

1. **Voice full-status stacking.** Right after intake, “What’s happening with my flyer?” listed several facts in one turn: no upload yet, production not assigned, work started, QA passed, email still being retried, Review is open. Direct questions later (“Has work started?”, “Is my flyer ready?”, “Did you keep my approval?”) stayed coherent. The stacked status sentence can sound like it disagrees with itself. Board wake can also produce the flyer while Maya is still on intake/materials, so some of those facts were already true. Still: the combined sentence is the weakest honesty moment on this walk.

2. **Board screenshots vs Voice.** Permanent sidebar **Review Room** is always there. That is not the same as “Review is ready.” During `qa_fail`, Voice refused to open Review; after `qa_pass` and after the failed notice, refund copy said **Make Me a Flyer — Ready for your review**. Some Board cards were still loading messages when the shot was taken. Durable proof for those beats is Voice + Machine record.

3. **Return-later phrasing.** First run Voice treated “Did you keep my approval?” as unknown. Scout mapped `approval` onto the existing approved-identity answer (not a new business rule). Retest: Voice reported `flyer-v2` and the pinned hash. Machine already had that pin.

4. **Payment** was the sealed paid fixture, not a new hosted Stripe click-through.
