## One action at a time

Stop after any step that fails the “What you should see” line. Take a screenshot using the rules at the bottom, then tell Scout which step number broke.

### 1. Open the link

Open the Studio link above.

**What you should see:** The Studio Lobby with a welcome film and a choice to begin.

### 2. Begin as a new visitor

Tap **LET’S GET STARTED**.

**What you should see:** The Conversation Room tablet. You should not need a computer.

### 3. Choose how you will talk

Tap **Use Voice guidance**, or type if the room is noisy.

**What you should see:** The Studio asks what you are trying to accomplish.

### 4. Answer as Mira

Speak or type these answers, one question at a time:

- What are you trying to accomplish? **Promote a spring sale with campaign graphics for Pine & Petal Market.**  
- Tell me a little about your business. **I run a small neighborhood market called Pine & Petal Market.**  
- When do you need this done? **Within a month.**  
- Is this a new project, or an update? **Brand new.**

If later questions appear, answer honestly as Mira. Say The Studio is a fit. Do not mention a carousel.

**What you should see:** The Studio recommends a route and then services.

### 5. Keep campaign graphics

If **Make My Campaign Graphics** is recommended, keep it. Confirm the Studio Plan when you are ready to pay.

**What you should see:** A checkout / pay step for this project. You should still be on your phone.

### 6. Sandbox pay — not a real charge

Check the terms box. Tap **Test pay with sandbox confirm**. Do **not** enter a card number. Do **not** open a real Stripe wallet if the screen asks you to pay with a live card.

**What you should see:** Payment finishes without taking real money. Intake should open next. If the test-pay button is missing, or the Studio sends you to enter a real card, stop and tell Scout.

**What you should see:** The Studio is ready to create an account / open the Board.

## Where to stop and report a problem

Stop immediately if any of these happen:

- The Studio link does not open.  
- A button, answer chip, checkbox, or upload control is too small or hidden.  
- You must change browser zoom to read or tap.  
- Text is cut off so the instruction is incomplete.  
- The keyboard covers the field and you cannot see what you are typing or submit.  
- Sandbox pay is missing, or a real card is required.  
- A refresh loses the project or signs you out unexpectedly.  
- A rejected or held file is treated as fully cleared.  
- You are told to finish a required step on a computer.  
- Carousel is offered as something you must buy.

Write the step number, what you tapped, and what you saw instead.
