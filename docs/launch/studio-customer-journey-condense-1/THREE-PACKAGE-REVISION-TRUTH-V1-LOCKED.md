## One round

One revision round is one complete list of requested changes within the original package scope.

A revision round may include reasonable changes within the original package scope, such as wording, a call-to-action adjustment, pacing or trim refinements, caption corrections, layout refinements, visual refinements within the approved direction, a clip swap to an already-ranked suitable alternate where applicable, music or B-roll adjustment or removal where applicable, and tightening an ending.

A revision round does not include a new creative direction, a new project replacing the original project, unrelated new source material replacing the original source, extra deliverables beyond the package scope, a major scope expansion, or switching to a different package.
