## Target (from Tagia sheet)

- Warm, calm, confident — alive and intentional  
- **Not** hard cartoon angles, nose, face outline, or floating emoji energy  

### Glasses
- Larger soft rounded rectangles  
- **Thinner top rim** than sides/bottom  
- Circular bridge with **white S**  

### Eyes
- Set **lower** in the lenses  
- Breathing room under top rim  
- More sclera visible — relaxed, real  

### Brows
- Soft, natural, slightly curved — separate from frames  

### Mouth
- Mostly hidden  
- Appears only when speaking  

### Lobby size
- About **140–160px** wide · `pointer-events: none`
